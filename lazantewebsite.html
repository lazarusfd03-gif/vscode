About Me

Music has always been more than just sound to me - it is emotion, atmosphere, storytelling, and experience. My journey combines creative composition, sound engineering, and branding, allowing me to understand not only how music should sound, but also how it should make people feel.

I specialize in cinematic, orchestral, and emotionally driven music production with a strong focus on realism, depth, and immersive sound design. Using industry-standard tools like Cubase, Nuendo, and Kontakt, I create compositions that blend modern production techniques with orchestral expression.

"Again X Gurenge" Cinematic Anime Orchestration Remix

Inspired by the songs Again by YUI and Gurenge by LiSA

Project Overview
A cinematic orchestration remix where two blockbuster anime opening themes were creatively merged into a single musical experience to imagine how both worlds would feel if the characters existed within the same universe. The project was designed with a strong cinematic narrative approach, combining emotional storytelling, orchestral intensity, and modern hybrid production techniques to create a seamless crossover experience for anime audiences.

Watch on YouTube

AC/DC - Thunderstruck (Orchestration Cover)

Inspired by Thunderstruck by the legendary rock band AC/DC

Project Overview
A cinematic orchestration cover of Thunderstruck, reimagining one of rock music's most iconic tracks into an epic hybrid orchestral experience. The project focused on transforming the raw energy of AC/DC's original composition into a large-scale cinematic production using orchestral scoring, aggressive percussion, trailer-style sound design, and hybrid arrangement techniques while preserving the instantly recognizable intensity of the original song.
